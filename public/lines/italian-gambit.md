## Notes
White plays an immediate `4. d4` in the Giuoco Piano to blast open central files and diagonals before Black can castle. You sacrifice a pawn for rapid development, open lines against `f7`, and concrete tactical threats, aiming to convert the initiative into a winning attack or superior endgame structure.

## Main Line: 4...exd4 5. O-O (Scotch Gambit Transposition)

eco: C50
playerColor: white

After Black captures on `d4`, White castles immediately instead of recapturing, inviting Black into sharp tactical waters where the `e`-file opens fast and `f7` comes under heavy fire.

```moves
e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Bc5 d4 exd4 O-O Nf6 e5 d5 exf6 dxc4 Re1+ Be6 Ng5 Qd5 Nc3 Qf5 Nce4 O-O-O g4 Qe5 Nxe6 fxe6 fxg7 Rhg8 Bh6
```

## Bishop Captures: 4...Bxd4 (Greco / Exchange Line)

eco: C50
playerColor: white

If Black takes with the bishop to avoid opening the `e`-file, White swaps the strong dark-squared bishop and builds a dominant central pawn duo (`c3` and `e4`) with easy development.

```moves
e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Bc5 d4 Bxd4 Nxd4 Nxd4 O-O Nf6 f4 d6 fxe5 dxe5 Bg5 Qe7 Nc3 c6 Kh1 Be6 Bxe6 Qxe6 Bxf6 gxf6 Qh5 O-O-O Rf2
```

## Knight Captures: 4...Nxd4 (Trap-Filled Line)

eco: C50
playerColor: white

Capturing with the knight looks solid, but taking `e5` sets up classic tactical tricks around `f7` and `Qg4`; solid play leads into an active middlegame where White holds the bishop pair and central space.

```moves
e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Bc5 d4 Nxd4 Nxd4 Bxd4 c3 Bb6 Qd5 Qe7 O-O Nf6 Qd3 d6 Bg5 h6 Bh4 g5 Bg3 Nh5 Nd2 Nf4 Bxf4 gxf4 a4 a6
```

## Declined: 4...Bb6 (Safe Retreat)

eco: C50
playerColor: white

Black refuses to open the center and drops the bishop back to safety. White gains a clear space advantage, locks down central squares, and transitions smoothly into a favorable middlegame or endgame.

```moves
e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Bc5 d4 Bb6 dxe5 Qe7 Nc3 Nxe5 Nxe5 Qxe5 Qe2 Nf6 Bd2 O-O O-O-O d6 f4 Qe7 h3 Be6 g4 Bxc4 Qxc4 Qe6 Qxe6 fxe6
```

## Declined: 4...d6 (Philidor-Style Setup)

eco: C50
playerColor: white

Black defends `e5` passively with `...d6`, allowing White to resolve the tension on favorable terms—either trading into an open endgame with a lead in development or pushing `d5` to cramp Black's pieces.

```moves
e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Bc5 d4 d6 dxc5 dxc5 Qxd8+ Nxd8 Nxe5 Be6 Nc3 Bxc4 Nxc4 Ne6 Be3 O-O-O Rd1 Rxd1+ Kxd1 Nf6 f3 Rd8+ Kc1
```

## Counter-Thrust: 4...Nf6 (Two Knights Transposition)

eco: C55
playerColor: white

Black ignores the `d4` tension to counterattack `e4` immediately. White pushes `dxe5` or `e5`, leading to dynamic piece play where Black's bishop on `c5` can become a target for `b4` or `Be3`.

```moves
e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Bc5 d4 Nf6 dxe5 Nxe4 Bxf7+ Kxf7 Qd5+ Kf8 Qxe4 d5 exd6 Qxd6 O-O Qe6 Nc3 Qxe4 Nxe4 Bb6 Bf4 Bf5 Rfe1 Re8
```
