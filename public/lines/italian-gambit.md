## Notes
White plays an immediate `4. d4` in the Giuoco Piano to blast open central files and diagonals before Black can castle. You sacrifice a pawn for rapid development, open lines against `f7`, and concrete tactical threats, aiming to convert the initiative into a winning attack or superior endgame structure.

## Scotch Gambit Transposition

eco: C50
playerColor: white

Idea: After 4...exd4, White castles immediately (5. O-O) instead of recapturing to open the e-file and seize rapid initiative.
Plan: Push 6. e5, sacrifice on f6 to disrupt Black's kingside, activate Re1+, and swarm Black's queen and king with Ng5, Nc3, and Nce4.
Watch Out: Stay sharp when Black answers 6. e5 with ...d5 and queenside castling—keep pieces coordinated so g4 and Bh6 stay backed up.

```moves
e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Bc5 d4 exd4 O-O Nf6 e5 d5 exf6 dxc4 Re1+ Be6 Ng5 Qd5 Nc3 Qf5 Nce4 O-O-O g4 Qe5 Nxe6 fxe6 fxg7 Rhg8 Bh6
```

## Bishop Captures: 4...Bxd4 (Greco / Exchange Line)

eco: C50
playerColor: white

Idea: Trade off Black's active dark-squared bishop on d4 and castle quickly while keeping an open f-file for the rook.
Plan: Break with f4 to exchange central pawns, pin the knight with Bg5, double-target Black's shattered kingside with Bxf6, and swing Qh5 and Rf2.
Watch Out: Tuck the king safely to Kh1 before opening diagonals, and guard against Black's ...O-O-O queenside counterplay.

```moves
e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Bc5 d4 Bxd4 Nxd4 Nxd4 O-O Nf6 f4 d6 fxe5 dxe5 Bg5 Qe7 Nc3 c6 Kh1 Be6 Bxe6 Qxe6 Bxf6 gxf6 Qh5 O-O-O Rf2
```

## Knight Captures: 4...Nxd4 (Trap-Filled Line)

eco: C50
playerColor: white

Idea: Exchange knights on d4, gain tempo with c3 against the bishop, and strike immediately with Qd5 to threaten f7 and e5.
Plan: Castle kingside, pin the f6 knight with Bg5, reroute Nd2, and expand on the queenside with a4 after trading the active f4 knight.
Watch Out: Don't get tricked by 4...Nxd4 tactics—retreat Qd3 safely after ...Nf6 and avoid letting Black open the g-file without compensation.

```moves
e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Bc5 d4 Nxd4 Nxd4 Bxd4 c3 Bb6 Qd5 Qe7 O-O Nf6 Qd3 d6 Bg5 h6 Bh4 g5 Bg3 Nh5 Nd2 Nf4 Bxf4 gxf4 a4 a6
```

## Declined: 4...Bb6 (Safe Retreat)

eco: C50
playerColor: white

Idea: When Black declines the center with 4...Bb6, capture dxe5 to gain central space and target the exposed queen on e5.
Plan: Develop Nc3, Bd2, and O-O-O, then advance f4, h3, and g4 on the kingside before trading into a space-rich endgame.
Watch Out: Keep e4 defended against ...Nxe4 and ...Qxe4 tricks and prevent Black's bishops from sniping your queenside castled king.

```moves
e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Bc5 d4 Bb6 dxe5 Qe7 Nc3 Nxe5 Nxe5 Qxe5 Qe2 Nf6 Bd2 O-O O-O-O d6 f4 Qe7 h3 Be6 g4 Bxc4 Qxc4 Qe6 Qxe6 fxe6
```

## Declined: 4...d6 (Philidor-Style Setup)

eco: C50
playerColor: white

Idea: Punish the passive 4...d6 defense with dxc5 and Qxd8+, forcing an early queen trade where White keeps better structure and activity.
Plan: Snatch the e5 pawn, trade off Black's active bishop with Nc3 and Nxc4, contest the d-file with Rd1, and stabilize with f3 and Kc1.
Watch Out: Watch for ...Be6 and ...O-O-O counterpressure along the d-file; keep your king safe on c1 after trading rooks.

```moves
e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Bc5 d4 d6 dxc5 dxc5 Qxd8+ Nxd8 Nxe5 Be6 Nc3 Bxc4 Nxc4 Ne6 Be3 O-O-O Rd1 Rxd1+ Kxd1 Nf6 f3 Rd8+ Kc1
```

## Counter-Thrust: 4...Nf6 (Two Knights Transposition)

eco: C55
playerColor: white

Idea: Punish 4...Nf6 and 5...Nxe4 immediately with the classic Bxf7+! fork followed by Qd5+ to strip Black of castling rights.
Plan: Recapture Qxe4, clarify the center with exd6, castle quickly, trade queens on e4, and target Black's uncoordinated king with Bf4 and Rfe1.
Watch Out: Be ready for Black's ...d5 counterstrike and ensure your e4 knight stays well supported against ...Bf5 and ...Re8.

```moves
e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Bc5 d4 Nf6 dxe5 Nxe4 Bxf7+ Kxf7 Qd5+ Kf8 Qxe4 d5 exd6 Qxd6 O-O Qe6 Nc3 Qxe4 Nxe4 Bb6 Bf4 Bf5 Rfe1 Re8
```
