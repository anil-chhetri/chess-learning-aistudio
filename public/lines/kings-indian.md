## Notes
A hypermodern defense where Black lets White build a broad pawn center before striking back with ...e5 or ...c5 and launching a kingside attack.

## Classical Variation

eco: E92
playerColor: black

Idea: Let White claim central space with d5, then strike back on the kingside using the locked center as a shield.
Plan: Reroute the knight via ...Ne7 to clear the f-pawn, push ...f5, and launch a kingside pawn storm against White's castled king.
Watch Out: Don't ignore White's queenside b4-c5 break—time ...f5 cleanly before White cracks open the c-file.

```moves
d4 Nf6 c4 g6 Nc3 Bg7 e4 d6 Nf3 O-O Be2 e5 O-O Nc6 d5 Ne7
```

## Samisch Variation

eco: E81
playerColor: black

Idea: White reinforces e4 with f3 to stop ...Ng4, so Black shifts to light-square control and fast kingside counterplay.
Plan: Strike the center with ...e5 and jump ...Nh5 to prepare ...f5 and challenge White's dark-squared bishop setup.
Watch Out: Watch out for White's Bh6 and g4-h4 pawn storm if White castles queenside after Qd2.

```moves
d4 Nf6 c4 g6 Nc3 Bg7 e4 d6 f3 O-O Be3 e5 d5 Nh5 Qd2
```

## Four Pawns Attack

eco: E76
playerColor: black

Idea: White overextends with f4 to build a massive pawn wall, giving Black immediate targets to undermine.
Plan: Attack the base and center at once with ...c5 and ...e6, opening files for the g7 bishop and queenside pieces.
Watch Out: Avoid passive moves that let White consolidate e4-d5-f4; challenge the pawn chain immediately.

```moves
d4 Nf6 c4 g6 Nc3 Bg7 e4 d6 f4 O-O Nf3 c5 d5 e6
```
