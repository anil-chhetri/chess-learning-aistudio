## Notes
A hypermodern defense where Black lets White build a broad pawn center before striking back with ...e5 or ...c5 and launching a kingside attack.

## Classical Variation

eco: E92
playerColor: black

White closes the center with d5 and Black reroutes the knight via ...Ne7 to prepare the thematic ...f5 kingside pawn break.

```moves
d4 Nf6 c4 g6 Nc3 Bg7 e4 d6 Nf3 O-O Be2 e5 O-O Nc6 d5 Ne7
```

## Samisch Variation

eco: E81
playerColor: black

White reinforces e4 with f3 to stop ...Ng4. Black responds with ...e5 and ...Nh5 to contest f4 and target the light squares.

```moves
d4 Nf6 c4 g6 Nc3 Bg7 e4 d6 f3 O-O Be3 e5 d5 Nh5 Qd2
```

## Four Pawns Attack

eco: E76
playerColor: black

White overextends with f4 to seize space. Black immediately undermines the pawn chain with ...c5 and ...e6.

```moves
d4 Nf6 c4 g6 Nc3 Bg7 e4 d6 f4 O-O Nf3 c5 d5 e6
```
