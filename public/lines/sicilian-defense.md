## Notes
Black fights for an asymmetrical position on move 1 by contesting d4 from the flank with 1...c5, trading a wing pawn for White's central d-pawn.

## Najdorf

eco: B90
playerColor: black

Idea: Play 5...a6 to take away b5 from White's pieces and keep maximum flexibility across both wings.
Plan: Challenge the center immediately with ...e5 to drive back the d4 knight, then develop ...Be6 and expand on the queenside.
Watch Out: Guard the d5 hole and watch White's f3-g4-h4 English Attack setup when White plays Be3.

```moves
e4 c5 Nf3 d6 d4 cxd4 Nxd4 Nf6 Nc3 a6 Be3 e5 Nb3 Be6
```

## Dragon

eco: B70
playerColor: black

Idea: Fianchetto the dark-squared bishop to g7 so it slices down the long h8-a1 diagonal toward White's queenside.
Plan: Castle kingside, develop ...Nc6, and generate fast counterplay down the half-open c-file against White's c3 knight and queenside.
Watch Out: White's Yugoslav Attack (Be3, f3, Qd2, O-O-O) aims for Bh6 and h4-h5—create queenside counterplay without delay.

```moves
e4 c5 Nf3 d6 d4 cxd4 Nxd4 Nf6 Nc3 g6 Be3 Bg7 f3 O-O Qd2 Nc6
```

## Classical

eco: B56
playerColor: black

Idea: Develop both knights naturally and hold a flexible ...d6/...e6 center without creating early pawn weaknesses.
Plan: Break the Bg5 pin with ...Be7, castle safely kingside, and counterattack White's queenside castled king along the c-file.
Watch Out: Respect White's Richter-Rauzer pressure (Bg5, Qd2, O-O-O) on d6 and avoid loosening your kingside pawns too early.

```moves
e4 c5 Nf3 d6 d4 cxd4 Nxd4 Nf6 Nc3 Nc6 Bg5 e6 Qd2 Be7 O-O-O O-O
```
