## Notes
Black fights for an asymmetrical position on move 1 by contesting d4 from the flank with 1...c5, trading a wing pawn for White's central d-pawn.

## Najdorf

eco: B90
playerColor: black

Black plays 5...a6 to prevent Bb5+ and prepare queenside expansion with ...b5 or a central ...e5 push without allowing Nb5.

```moves
e4 c5 Nf3 d6 d4 cxd4 Nxd4 Nf6 Nc3 a6 Be3 e5 Nb3 Be6
```

## Dragon

eco: B70
playerColor: black

Black fianchettoes the dark-squared bishop to g7 to pressure the long h8-a1 diagonal and counterattack down the half-open c-file.

```moves
e4 c5 Nf3 d6 d4 cxd4 Nxd4 Nf6 Nc3 g6 Be3 Bg7 f3 O-O Qd2 Nc6
```

## Classical

eco: B56
playerColor: black

Black develops naturally with ...Nc6 and ...e6, keeping a flexible pawn structure while preparing counterplay on the dark squares.

```moves
e4 c5 Nf3 d6 d4 cxd4 Nxd4 Nf6 Nc3 Nc6 Bg5 e6 Qd2 Be7 O-O-O O-O
```
